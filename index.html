FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account to use Streamza?

No. Streamza works without any sign-up. You connect to streaming platforms using a stream key from that platform — your data stays on your device.

Which platforms can I stream to?

YouTube, Facebook, Twitch, Kick, Restream, and any service that accepts a Custom RTMP / RTMPS stream key.

Where do I find my stream key?

In your platform's live dashboard — for example, YouTube Studio → Go Live → Stream, or your Twitch Creator Dashboard → Settings → Stream.

Is Streamza free?

Yes! Streamza gives you 15 minutes free with absolutely no credit card required and no account needed. You can try all core features — live streaming, RTMP, and screen recording — completely free. A PRO subscription unlocks unlimited streaming time, 60 fps, 1440p (2K) quality, higher bitrate, studio audio, and no watermark.

Does Streamza collect my data?

We do not collect your personal data or your stream content on our servers. Your broadcast goes directly to the platform you choose.

Can I stream without installing the app?

Yes. Streamza Web Studio runs in your browser — upload a video, paste your RTMP key, and it broadcasts from the cloud. No app or download needed.

Can I run a 24/7 looping live stream?

Yes. Turn on 'Loop the video forever' in Web Studio (or the app's video-file mode) to broadcast a clip non-stop — ideal for music, radio, and info channels.

What is RTMP streaming?

RTMP (Real-Time Messaging Protocol) is the industry standard technology used for transmitting video and audio over the internet to live streaming platforms.

How do I stream to YouTube from my phone?

Open Streamza, select YouTube, paste your YouTube stream key, choose your camera or screen, and tap Go Live.

Can I stream to a Facebook Group?

Yes. Just create a live video in your Facebook Group, copy the RTMP stream key and server URL, and paste it into Streamza.

Does Streamza support multistreaming?

Streamza connects to multistreaming services like Restream.io. By streaming to Restream through Streamza, you can broadcast to multiple platforms simultaneously.

Can I stream mobile games?

Yes, Streamza has a built-in HD screen recorder perfect for streaming mobile games directly to Twitch or YouTube.

What is adaptive bitrate?

Adaptive bitrate is a feature that automatically adjusts your video quality in real-time based on your internet connection speed to prevent your stream from disconnecting.

Can I add a facecam to my screen recording?

Yes, you can enable your front camera as a facecam overlay while sharing your screen.

What happens if my internet disconnects?

Streamza will attempt to reconnect automatically. Our adaptive bitrate helps prevent disconnects by lowering the quality before a drop occurs.

Do you add a watermark to my stream?

The free version of Streamza may include a small, unobtrusive watermark. Upgrading to PRO removes the watermark completely.

Can I stream a pre-recorded video as if it was live?

Absolutely. You can select a video file from your device and Streamza will broadcast it to your chosen platform as a live stream.

What is WebRTC vs RTMP?

WebRTC is designed for ultra-low latency browser-to-browser communication (like video calls). RTMP is designed for reliable, high-quality broadcasting to platforms like YouTube and Twitch. Streamza utilizes RTMP for broadcasting.

How much data does live streaming use?

It depends on your bitrate. Streaming at 1080p (4500 kbps) uses approximately 2 GB of data per hour.

Can I schedule streams?

Stream scheduling is handled on the platform side (e.g., YouTube Studio). Once you schedule it there, you use the provided stream key in Streamza when it's time to go live.

Does Streamza use OBS?

No, Streamza is a standalone mobile app and browser-based web studio. It replaces the need for OBS when streaming from a phone or using our cloud studio.

Can I stream forever?

Yes! If you use the Streamza Web Studio or stream a video file from the app, you can loop it 24/7 indefinitely.

Is there a PC or Mac version of Streamza?

Currently, Streamza is an Android app and a web-based studio accessible from any desktop browser.

How do I fix dropped frames?

Dropped frames are usually caused by a weak internet connection. Enable 'Adaptive Bitrate' in Streamza or manually lower your resolution and bitrate.

Can I stream vertical video (Shorts/Reels)?

Yes, if you hold your phone vertically, Streamza will broadcast a vertical video signal, perfect for platforms supporting vertical live streams.

What resolution does Streamza support?

Streamza supports up to 1080p on the free version, and up to 1440p (2K) on the PRO version, device permitting.

Can I record my stream while broadcasting?

Yes, Streamza can save a local copy of your broadcast directly to your device storage.

Do I need 50 subscribers to stream on YouTube with Streamza?

No! Because Streamza uses a custom RTMP connection instead of the mobile YouTube API, you can stream to YouTube regardless of your subscriber count.

How do I contact support?

You can email us at probrostraders@gmail.com or visit our Support page for more detailed guides.

Can I use Streamza on iOS (iPhone)?

The Streamza app is currently Android-only, but you can use the Streamza Web Studio from the Safari browser on any device.
