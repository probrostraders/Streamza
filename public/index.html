FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to install anything?

No. Streamza Studio runs entirely in your browser — upload a video, paste your stream key, and we broadcast it from the cloud.

Which platforms can I stream to?

YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Kick, Restream, and any service that accepts a Custom RTMP / RTMPS stream key.

What video format do I need?

An MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio (the default export from most tools). We stream-copy it without re-encoding, so it goes live instantly and stays high quality.

Can it run 24/7?

Yes — turn on "Loop the video forever" and it broadcasts non-stop. During the free beta one stream runs at a time; Pro will add multiple simultaneous streams.

Is it really free?

Yes, free during beta. Pro plans (more streams, priority, scheduling) arrive as we scale — join the waitlist to get early-bird pricing.
