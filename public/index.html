FAQ

Questions, answered

How do the 5 slots work?

There are 5 streaming slots. You claim a free one, and it's yours for 24 hours. When all 5 are busy, you wait for one to free up. At 24h your slot stops automatically and you can renew.

What does it cost?

Free during beta. At launch, a slot is $1 for 24 hours — pay only for the days you stream.

Which platforms can I stream to?

YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Kick, Restream, and any service with a Custom RTMP / RTMPS key.

What video format do I need?

An MP4 with H.264 video + AAC audio (the default export from most tools). We stream-copy it — no re-encode — so it goes live instantly.

What happens after 24 hours?

The stream stops and the slot frees for others. You'll be prompted to renew (claim again) to continue.
