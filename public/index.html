FAQ

Questions, answered

How do the 10 slots work?

There are 10 streaming slots. Tap any free slot to start a free 15-minute trial. To keep streaming, subscribe for full 24-hour slots. When all 10 are busy, you wait for one to free up.

What does it cost?

Every slot is free for the first 15 minutes — no card needed. To stream the full 24 hours, subscribe monthly and stream as often as you like. Cancel anytime.

Which platforms can I stream to?

YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Kick, Restream, and any service with a Custom RTMP / RTMPS key.

What video format do I need?

An MP4 with H.264 video + AAC audio (the default export from most tools). We stream-copy it — no re-encode — so it goes live instantly.

What happens after the free trial?

At 15 minutes the stream stops and the slot frees for others. Subscribe while you're live to switch to a full 24-hour slot and keep streaming non-stop.

Can I stream a pre-recorded video using just a URL and stream key?

Yes. Upload your video file, paste the RTMP URL and stream key from YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Kick or any custom RTMP server, and it goes live immediately.

Do I need OBS or any software installed?

No. Streamza Web Studio runs entirely in your browser and streams from the cloud — no OBS, no software install, and no PC needs to stay on.

Can the video loop 24/7 automatically?

Yes. Turn on Loop the video and Streamza replays it seamlessly, non-stop, for as long as your slot or subscription is active.

Where do I get my RTMP URL and stream key?

Every platform provides one in its live-streaming dashboard — for example YouTube Studio → Go Live → Stream, or Twitch Creator Dashboard → Settings → Stream.
